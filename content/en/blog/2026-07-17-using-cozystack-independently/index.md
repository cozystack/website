![Using Cozystack independently? We are looking for independent adopters for the CNCF Incubation review.](using-cozystack-independently.png)

As part of the CNCF Incubation review, the Technical Oversight Committee (TOC) is speaking with Cozystack adopters about their real-world experience.

We are especially looking for teams and individual adopters using Cozystack **outside a commercial engagement with Ænix**. If that is you, we would love to hear how you run it, what works, and what does not.

## How to take part

If you are using Cozystack independently, tell the maintainers in [GitHub
Discussions](https://github.com/cozystack/cozystack/discussions). If you would
rather not say so in public, reach us in the project's Slack or Telegram
channels linked below and we will take it from there.

**This is not a sales call. Interviews can remain confidential.**
